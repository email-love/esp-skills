# Security

## What these skills do

The skills in this repo are reference documents. They contain no executable code, make no network requests, and require no credentials or tool access. They tell an AI assistant what a platform's templating language supports; they do not connect to your ESP.

Nothing you paste into a conversation is sent anywhere by these skills. Normal care applies to what you share with your AI provider — template code often contains real customer field names, and occasionally real customer data in a pasted preview.
